Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-10

This Privacy Policy describes how HotspotUV (“the Software”), developed by 3Design DK (“we”, “us”), handles data in connection with its use as a plugin for Autodesk 3ds Max.

Data we collect

HotspotUV does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The Software runs locally within your installation of Autodesk 3ds Max and operates only on the 3D scene data and files you choose to work with on your own computer. We do not gather names, email addresses, IP addresses, usage analytics, telemetry, or any other personal or identifying information.

How data is used

Because HotspotUV does not collect personal data, we do not use, process, or profile any personal information. All operations performed by the Software (such as reading UV data, trim sheets, and applying UV mapping) happen locally on your machine and remain under your control.

Third parties

HotspotUV does not share any data with third parties, because it does not collect any. The Software does not integrate with external analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Data retention and deletion

As HotspotUV does not collect or store personal data, there is no personal data to retain or delete. Any files or project data you create remain solely on your own system and under your control.

Your rights

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data associated with you held by us. If you have any questions about this policy or about data handling in HotspotUV, you can contact us at any time.
