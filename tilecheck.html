TileCheck needs a mouse

This one is desktop only — not a smaller screen away, a different input away. Driving the preview takes buttons a touchscreen doesn’t have:

  • middle mouse button to orbit the camera
  • right mouse button to swing the light across the surface
  • scroll wheel to zoom, and a window wide enough to judge a seam

Open 3designdk.com/tilecheck.html on a desktop and drop your maps straight onto the page.
